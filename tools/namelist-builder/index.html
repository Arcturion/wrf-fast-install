WRF Namelist Builder

Design domains interactively, pick physics from the dropdowns, and download a matched namelist.wps + namelist.input pair. Everything runs in your browser — no server, no upload.

1 · Projection & parent domain

The reference point is the centre of domain 1. Grid sizes are set per domain in the cards below.

2 · Domains

Drag a nest on the map, or type its centre. Nest grid sizes snap so (size − 1) ÷ ratio stays a whole number, as WRF requires.

3 · Run & WPS settings

Time step guideline: roughly 6 s per km of parent dx. Metgrid levels must match the meteorological input data you will feed to WPS (for example 34 for typical GFS files).

4 · Physics (parameterisation)

Presets from the WRF Users Guide:

Physics can differ per domain — most often you will switch cumulus off (0) on domains finer than about 4 km.
